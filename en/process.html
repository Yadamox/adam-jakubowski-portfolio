How I work

First, the goal and boundaries. Then planning, delivery coordination and closure of the agreed scope.

From brief to handover

  1. 01 / Define the destination

    Goal, scope, current status, budget and dates. I check feasibility and conflicts of interest.

  2. 02 / Agree the plan and roles

    Schedule, dependencies, responsibilities, decisions and reporting. My role and its boundaries are confirmed in writing.

  3. 03 / Coordinate delivery

    I coordinate the agreed work and parties, monitor progress, risks and open decisions. Scope changes require agreement.

  4. 04 / Close the scope

    Prepare handover, snag lists, remedial arrangements and documentation. Coordination is not an independent assessment of my own work.

I currently accept non-binding enquiries only. Until the formal requirements are completed, I do not enter into paid contracts, accept payments or reserve project dates.

Clear responsibilities

We confirm the project management scope, decision authority, contracting parties, responsibilities, dates and remedial arrangements in writing. Coordination does not mean assuming all contractors’ responsibilities. Designers and contractors remain responsible for their agreed work; a cooperating contractor measures, manufactures and installs furniture. Transport, carrying inside, protection and unloading are agreed separately. I do not replace a statutory construction manager, licensed designer or expert surveyor, and do not provide legal advice or regulated expert assessments.

I remain responsible for my own agreed scope; this description does not exclude liability required by law.
