Landbyska Verket

Delivery of bespoke restaurant furnishings in Stockholm — from site measurements and production coordination to delivery and installation.

RoleLead Project Manager
OrganisationSTOLPIN
PeriodFebruary–April 2026
LocationStockholm, Sweden

Role and responsibility

One coordination point between the client and delivery.

I participated in the Landbyska Verket restaurant project as Lead Project Manager on behalf of the bespoke joinery contractor, STOLPIN.

I was responsible for day-to-day client communication, schedule coordination, site measurements, and monitoring production and delivery dates. I also participated in selected price negotiations.

STOLPIN's delivery scope included:

  • furniture and bespoke joinery
  • sofas and tables
  • doors and wall panelling
  • structural ceiling elements
  • a multi-material bar
  • delivery and installation
