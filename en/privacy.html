Privacy policy

Controller and contact

The data controller is Adam Jakubowski, Konin, Poland. Contact: info@adam-jakubowski.com.

How the site works

The site is hosted and protected by Cloudflare Pages. It uses no advertising accounts, analytics tools or optional cookies. Fields on the contact pages remain locally in the browser and only prepare a message in the user’s email application. The site does not transmit enquiry content to an external form operator.

Purposes and legal bases

Data voluntarily sent by email are used to respond, assess fit and capacity, check a potential conflict of interest and take requested steps before a possible contract under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Necessary correspondence records, security and legal-claim protection may rely on legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f); legal obligations on Article 6(1)(c). Providing data is voluntary, but without contact details and a description of the matter a response may be impossible.

Recipients and transfers

Data may be processed by the email provider and necessary IT services. Cloudflare may process technical connection data such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the site. Providers may process data outside the EEA using mechanisms required by the GDPR, including Standard Contractual Clauses. Data are not sold.

Retention

Enquiries that do not lead to cooperation are generally removed after 6 months. Correspondence concerning an active matter is kept for up to 12 months after closure; contract or legally required records are retained for the period required by law or needed to protect claims. Cloudflare technical logs follow that provider’s retention periods.

Rights

You may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction or portability and object to processing. Where consent is the basis, it may be withdrawn. You may complain to the Polish data protection authority. I do not use automated decision-making or profiling.
