Do you design furniture or interiors?

No. I review and control an agreed delivery scope; I do not replace the designer or contractor.

Do you guarantee cost, timing or quality?

No. I document status, risks and decisions in the written scope, but do not guarantee outcomes controlled by other parties.

What is the B2C service area?

Konin + 50 km as the primary area. Travel beyond it is agreed individually.

Can I send confidential B2B documents?

Not before fit and conflict screening. The initial form does not accept attachments.

Does the form book a date?

No. It starts qualification only. Scope, capacity, price and terms are confirmed separately in writing.
