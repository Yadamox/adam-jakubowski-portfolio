FAQ

Can you manage the whole project, not only furniture?

External project management for commercial interiors, shops, showrooms and premises. Scope, schedule, contractor coordination, deliveries and decisions through to handover — within the agreed role. Beyond the furniture package. Scope and pricing agreed individually.

Do you offer bespoke furniture?

I can organise measurement, manufacture and installation of kitchens, wardrobes and fitted furniture with a contractor. Scope and roles are agreed individually.

Can you arrange transport?

Yes, with a carrier. Access, carrying inside, protection and unloading are agreed separately.

Will you independently inspect your own work?

No. Independent advice and handover assistance are only for another contractor’s work, not projects I deliver or organise myself.

Can you provide a larger installation crew?

A larger installation requires confirmation of scope and contractor availability before execution.

What do new services cost?

Furniture, installation and coordination are priced individually. Transport may be included in the project. Existing independent support prices apply only to another contractor’s work.

Does an enquiry reserve a date?

No. It is a non-binding discussion, without a paid contract, payment or reservation.

I currently accept non-binding enquiries only. Until the formal requirements are completed, I do not enter into paid contracts, accept payments or reserve project dates.

Clear responsibilities

We confirm the project management scope, decision authority, contracting parties, responsibilities, dates and remedial arrangements in writing. Coordination does not mean assuming all contractors’ responsibilities. Designers and contractors remain responsible for their agreed work; a cooperating contractor measures, manufactures and installs furniture. Transport, carrying inside, protection and unloading are agreed separately. I do not replace a statutory construction manager, licensed designer or expert surveyor, and do not provide legal advice or regulated expert assessments.

I remain responsible for my own agreed scope; this description does not exclude liability required by law.
